Which rule is working: the scoreboard

Every past session in the tracker log is replayed. For each rule, take the stocks it would have picked that day and compare their move 2 and 4 weeks later with the average of every tracked stock (the "market"). The page then recommends the rule that is leading: if the Top 30 leads, the plan is a plain rotation into the Top 30; if another rule leads, the plan switches to it; if nothing beats the market, it says so. This re-runs every day as the log grows.

Where your money is

Rules: TRIM = bigger than the max-per-stock setting. REVIEW EXIT = closed below the 3 × ATR risk line from your average cost, a SELL signal, or a KDJ Exit. KEEP = picked today by the recommended rule or the runner-up. DON'T ADD = flagged NO DIP – DON'T CHASE. Option positions and cash-secured puts are not included.

3

Buy list

Sizing splits the budget equally across the BUY rows from the recommended rule (most 6), capped at the max-per-stock setting, in two entries: the latest close and Support (or one ATR lower if it is already under Support). Runner-up and 2-week rows are shown for reference and are not funded. Stocks with a SELL signal or NO DIP – DON'T CHASE are not funded. Earnings inside the next 10 days are flagged: a gap can skip straight past your entry.
